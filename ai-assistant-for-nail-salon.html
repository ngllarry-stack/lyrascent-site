What it handles for nail salons.

Services and hours

Manicures, pedicures, extensions and the rest of your menu, plus your hours and holiday closures, in your words.

Appointment requests

The service, a preferred day and time, and contact details, sent to you to confirm.

Walk-in questions

It explains how walk-ins work at your salon and never promises an open chair.

Your menu prices, if you want

It shares prices only if you put them in its facts. Otherwise it says the salon will confirm.

Chats in your customers' languages

Visitors can chat in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese or Tagalog. You get each request in English, with a short summary.

Every request in your inbox

Name, phone or email, what they need and when, in one tidy email, instead of a voicemail you have to decode.

What it never does.

  • It does not confirm openings.You confirm every appointment. It collects the request.
  • It does not give health or skin advice.Questions about allergies, reactions or infections go to you as a message. If someone is unwell it tells them to get medical help.
  • It does not handle complaints or refunds.Those become a message to you, marked urgent.
  • It never pretends to be a person.It says it is an AI assistant.

Questions nail salons ask.

Can it take appointments?

It collects the request with a preferred day and time and sends it to you. You confirm. It does not put anything on your calendar by itself.

Can it tell customers our prices?

Only if you put your menu prices in its facts. Otherwise it says the salon will confirm.

Can customers chat in Vietnamese or Spanish?

Yes. The chat works in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese and Tagalog, and you get each request in English with a short summary.
