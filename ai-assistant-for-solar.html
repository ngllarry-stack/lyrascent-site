What it handles for solar installers.

Service area and basics

It answers where you install and how the process works, using the wording you approve.

Questions you choose to ask

If you want to know whether they own the home or roughly when they would like to start, it asks. Only what you list.

Callback requests

A preferred day and time, a phone or email, and a note, sent to you.

Hand-off, not guesses

Anything about savings, incentives or contracts goes to you instead of being guessed at.

Chats in your customers' languages

Visitors can chat in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese or Tagalog. You get each request in English, with a short summary.

Every request in your inbox

Name, phone or email, what they need and when, in one tidy email, instead of a voicemail you have to decode.

What it never does.

  • It does not estimate savings or payback.Savings depend on the home, and incentives change.
  • It does not give tax or financial advice.It points those questions to you.
  • It does not promise install dates.It collects a preferred time. You confirm.
  • It never pretends to be a person.It says it is an AI assistant.
