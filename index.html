How it works, start to finish.

1. We learn your business

A short conversation about your services, service area, hours, prices and how you like to talk to customers. Your assistant only knows what you tell us.

2. We build and test it

We set up your assistant and test it with real customer questions before anyone outside sees it. You read it and approve what it says.

3. One line goes on your website

We give you a single line of code to paste into your site, and we can add it for you. A chat bubble then appears on your pages. If we build your website, it is already in.

4. Leads come straight to you

When a visitor leaves their details, you get an email with their name, how to reach them, what they need and when. Anything the assistant is unsure about comes to you too. You can switch it off at any time.
